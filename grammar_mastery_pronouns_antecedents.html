Master the basics of Pronouns & Antecedents

What is a Pronoun?

A pronoun is a word that takes the place of a noun so you don't have to keep repeating the same noun.

Examples: he, she, it, they, him, her, them, someone, who, which, their, standard.

What is an Antecedent?

An antecedent is the original noun (person, place, thing, or idea) that the pronoun refers back to or replaces. ("Ante" means before).

Example: "When Maya lost her key, she called home."
Maya = Antecedent • she = Pronoun.

Pronoun-Antecedent Agreement

A pronoun must match its antecedent in number (singular or plural) and gender!

  • Singular: The cat licked its paw.
  • Plural: The dogs wagged their tails.
  • Indefinite Pronouns: Words like everyone, somebody, nobody are singular. (e.g., "Everyone brought his or her pencil" or singular "their").
